About

Product-led.
Outcome-driven.

I started my career writing code — but I found myself more drawn to the questions behind the code: why are we building this? Who is it for? What will actually move the needle? That curiosity led me to make a deliberate move into product, and I've never looked back.

Over nearly seven years at Barclays, I've owned end-to-end delivery across some of the bank's most complex programmes — fraud prevention, identity verification, and dispute case management — spanning US and UK markets. I grew from Business Analyst to Associate Vice President in record time, becoming the youngest and fastest-promoted in my cohort.

What drives me isn't the title — it's ownership. The moment a half-baked problem becomes a funded roadmap, a funded roadmap becomes a live feature, and a live feature becomes a measurable outcome. I'm comfortable in ambiguity, confident with senior stakeholders, and relentless about closing the loop on every commitment.

I'm now actively exploring AI/ML product thinking — studying foundations in machine learning, RAG architectures, and LLMs — and looking for a role where I can bring that curiosity to scale.
